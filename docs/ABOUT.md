# About Fan7 — The Technician

Fan7 is an AI-native customer-experience and operational-intelligence platform for automotive service businesses.

## The Problem

Modern repair shops generate valuable information across repair orders, customer histories, service recommendations, technician activity, vehicle records, and communications. Much of that information remains fragmented inside operational systems and is difficult to turn into a consistent customer experience or a clear view of shop performance.

Fan7 is designed to connect those signals without replacing the shop-management software already running the business.

## The Product

Fan7 adds an intelligence and engagement layer across three connected audiences:

- **Owners and managers** receive clearer operational and performance insight.
- **Technicians and advisors** gain recognition, coaching signals, and useful context.
- **Customers** gain improved visibility into their service relationship and vehicle history.

The platform combines multi-tenant data integration, customer engagement, technician intelligence, Vehicle Passport records, operational analytics, and governed AI tooling.

## Real-World Development

Fan7 is being shaped through real automotive-service workflows. Its initial validation environment provides practical feedback about shop operations, technician performance, customer communication, and the information owners need to make better decisions.

This approach keeps product development connected to measurable business needs instead of hypothetical workflows.

## What Makes Fan7 Different

Fan7 does not attempt to become another repair-order system. It complements established shop-management platforms and focuses on the experiences and intelligence around the repair:

- customer trust and engagement;
- technician recognition and development;
- lifelong vehicle-service context;
- management visibility; and
- responsible access to operational intelligence through AI.

## Vision

Fan7's vision is to give independent automotive service businesses enterprise-grade customer engagement, technician intelligence, and responsible AI-powered insight without disrupting the workflows that already run the shop.
