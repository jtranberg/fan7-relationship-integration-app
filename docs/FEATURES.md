# Fan7 Features

This page separates available platform capabilities from planned expansion. Availability may vary by tenant, role, integration state, and rollout stage.

## Current Platform Foundation

- Multi-tenant SaaS architecture
- Per-tenant Shopmonkey integration
- Customer synchronization
- Repair-order synchronization
- Service-item synchronization
- JWT-secured authentication
- Role-based protected experiences
- Tenant-aware data access
- Customer portal foundation
- Vehicle Passport foundation
- Technician dashboard capability
- Secure Model Context Protocol foundation

## Customer Experience

Current product direction includes customer profile access, vehicle history, service timelines, digital communication, and engagement workflows.

The Vehicle Passport is designed to preserve useful service context around the vehicle while supporting future portability and ownership continuity.

## Technician Experience

Fan7's technician experience is designed to make performance visible and constructive through dashboards, experience points, levels, recognition, performance signals, and future coaching capabilities.

## Owner and Management Experience

Management capabilities bring together repair-order activity, parts and labor signals, technician productivity, operational dashboards, store metrics, and integration status.
