# Fan7 Trust Center

Fan7's Trust Center explains how the platform approaches security, privacy, responsible data use, platform governance, business continuity, accessibility, responsible AI, and lifelong Vehicle Passport records.

[Visit the live Fan7 Trust Center](https://fan7.netlify.app/trust)

![Fan7 Trust Center](../assets/screenshots/fan7-trust-center.png)

## Trust Principles

### Secure Authentication

Fan7 uses protected sessions, role-based access, and tenant-aware authorization to control access to platform experiences and information.

### Privacy First

Information collection and processing are intended to remain purpose-limited and connected to legitimate platform functions.

### Vehicle Portability

Vehicle Passport records are designed around long-term vehicle identity, useful service context, and future ownership continuity.

### Responsible AI

AI capabilities are designed to assist people, not replace professional judgment. Human oversight remains central to operational and customer decisions.

## Documentation Areas

The live Trust Center contains documentation covering:

- security and API security;
- incident response and vulnerability disclosure;
- backups, disaster recovery, and business continuity;
- privacy, processing, retention, and third-party services;
- governance, acceptable use, responsible AI, and release lifecycle;
- open-source licences and accessibility; and
- Vehicle Passport trust, portability, and future governance.

## Certification Statement

The Trust Center documents Fan7's current practices and product direction. It does not claim SOC 2, ISO 27001, or another third-party certification unless that certification is explicitly identified and verifiable.
