# Contribution Guidelines

Thank you for your interest in Fan7 — The Technician.

Fan7 is a proprietary product developed by App Intelligence.ca. This public repository exists as a product and engineering showcase; it is not an open-source development repository and does not accept production source-code contributions.

## Appropriate Contributions

We welcome thoughtful reports concerning:

- documentation errors;
- broken public links;
- accessibility concerns;
- unclear product descriptions; and
- responsible security disclosures submitted through the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Before Opening an Issue

Please confirm that the issue concerns material published in this repository and does not include:

- personal or customer information;
- vehicle identifiers;
- credentials, tokens, or private URLs;
- confidential business information; or
- unverified security details.

Feature proposals, partnerships, licensing enquiries, and pilot discussions should be directed to [App Intelligence.ca](https://appintelligence.ca/).

Submitting feedback does not transfer ownership of Fan7 or grant rights to its proprietary materials.
